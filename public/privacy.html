Privacy

Tasks · updated October 8, 2026 · en español

Tasks has no accounts, ads or analytics. We don’t collect personal data and we don’t sell anything to anyone. The only content that leaves your device is what you dictate, and only if you allow microphone access after reading where it goes (see Dictation), and the suggestions you choose to send (see Suggestions).

Your tasks and places

Tasks, sections, reminders and places are stored only on your device. On iPhone they are part of the device’s own backup (iCloud or your computer), which Apple manages, not us. You can export or delete them from Settings.

Calendar

If you connect it in Settings → Calendar, the app asks for access to the iPhone’s calendars to show your events in the Agenda and the widget. And only if you choose so in Settings → Calendar → Add my tasks, it adds your timed tasks as events to the calendar you pick (the “Tasks” one the app creates or one of yours; if it’s shared, whoever shares it will see them) and removes them when you check them off. It all happens on the iPhone itself: events aren’t stored in the app or its backups and are never sent to our server or anyone else (except what shows in the screenshot of a suggestion you choose to send). You can turn it off in Settings → Calendar or remove the permission in iPhone Settings.

Location

The app asks for your location only while you use it, and only for two things: saving where a place is and finding places near you. Reminders when you arrive at or leave a place are watched by iOS on the iPhone itself. Your location is never sent to our server.

Place searches (“Walmart”) use Apple Maps, subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Reminders

In the iPhone app, reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled on the device and never go through a server.

In the web version, so that a reminder arrives with the app closed, our server receives the time of each reminder and its content encrypted on your device with a key the server doesn’t have. The server never sees the text of your tasks.

What the server stores

To send reminders and limit abuse, the server stores per device: a random identifier, the hash of its access key, the notification address given by the browser (web only), an anonymized hash of the IP address and the date of last use. A device that isn’t used for 180 days is deleted automatically.

Dictation

The first time you tap the microphone, the app explains what is sent and where, and then the iPhone asks for microphone access. If you don’t allow it, nothing is sent: the microphone doesn’t record, and what you add with Siri is understood by the iPhone itself.

With permission, the audio and your device’s date and time are sent to our server to be transcribed with AI models from Cloudflare Workers AI (Whisper). When the app is in Spanish, the sentence is also interpreted there (Nemotron); in English it is interpreted on your device. If you add a task with Siri or Shortcuts, the iPhone has already transcribed it and only the text is sent (in Spanish only). Audio and text are processed on the spot and are not stored or logged. Your list of places is not sent: the name you say is matched with your places on the device.

Cloudflare, which hosts the server, processes this data only to provide the service to us: according to its terms, it doesn’t store it or use it to train models or for anything else (Workers AI data usage).

You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Dictation → Don’t send my voice.

Suggestions

From Settings → Suggest an improvement you can circle any part of the app and write what you’d change. It reaches our server only when you tap Send: your message, a screenshot of what was on the screen with what you circled (you can remove it before sending; without it, the text of your tasks isn’t sent), which screen you were on and technical details to understand it (app version, language, theme and device model). It doesn’t carry your name or anything that identifies you, except what you write.

Only the developer reads it, and deletes it once read; anything not deleted within a year is deleted automatically. To limit abuse, an anonymized fingerprint of the IP address is also stored.

Microphone

The microphone is only used while you dictate, when you turn it on.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn’t collect data from anyone.
