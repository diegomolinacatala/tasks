Frequently asked questions

Where are my tasks stored?

Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no sync. To move them to another device: in Tasks, Settings → Export backup, and on the other one, Import backup. If you delete the app, its tasks are deleted.

How do I switch the language?

In Tasks, Settings → Language: Automatic (your iPhone’s language), Español or English. The widgets follow the language you choose.

I don’t get reminders

iPhone Settings → Tasks → Notifications → turn on Allow Notifications. Also check that no Focus mode is silencing Tasks.

It doesn’t remind me when I arrive somewhere

The place needs a location (in Tasks, the Places tab) and Tasks needs location access While Using the App: iPhone Settings → Tasks → Location. The iPhone itself gives the reminder and may take a few minutes to notice you’ve arrived.

The microphone doesn’t work

iPhone Settings → Tasks → turn on Microphone. Dictation needs an internet connection. If you see “Dictation is used up for today”, the service’s daily limit has been reached and it works again the next day; meanwhile, you can type the task.

I don’t want my voice to leave the iPhone

In Tasks, Settings → Dictation → Don’t send my voice. Typing tasks and adding them with Siri keep working without sending anything. More details in the privacy policy.

How do I use it with Siri?

Say “Hey Siri, add to Tasks” and then the task. It’s added without opening the app. They are also in the Shortcuts app: Add task and Move overdue to today.
